# COMPLIANCE - Copy and Reply Rules

> **Source:** provided by the client 2026-08-11. Governs **every person who touches the inbox**, not just copywriters.
> Non-compliance creates exposure for every member of the network, not only for us.
> **This document outranks every other copy note in this folder.** Where a campaign doc and this doc disagree, this doc wins.

---

## 1. Never use these words or phrases

| Banned | Use instead |
|---|---|
| Eliminate / avoid / get out of capital gains tax | **Defer** or **reduce**. Never eliminate. |
| IRS-approved, IRS-supported | **IRS-compliant** |
| Guaranteed savings, or any promise of a result | Nothing. Do not promise outcomes. |
| 1031 rescue, or any suggestion of saving or fixing a failed exchange | Nothing. Do not reference failed exchanges at all. |
| Do not overpay the IRS | Nothing. |
| Lock in today's low tax rates | Nothing. See rate rule below. |
| Your money grows tax free | Nothing. |
| You control the trust / you call the shots / anything similar | See creditor's-right wording below. |
| Proprietary | Nothing. We do not own the structure and cannot claim it. |
| Any dollar figure attached to savings | Nothing. **No exceptions.** |

## 2. Terminology, when we do get specific

- The seller **transfers the asset to the trust**. The **trust sells it to the buyer.**
  (Not: "the proceeds go into a structure," which describes the seller selling and then funding a trust.)
- **Sales proceeds.** Not money, cash, or funds.
- **Repayments.** Not distributions or payouts.
- The **promissory note is secured by the trust assets.** The seller is **not investing or reinvesting.**
- The seller has a **creditor's right to approve the trust's investments.** The seller does **not control** the trust.
- Taxes are paid **at the rate in effect at the time of constructive receipt.** Never imply a locked rate.

## 3. House rules

1. **Name no structures in cold copy.** Say there are **more options than the two the client has been told about.**
2. **Say property, not building.** A third of this audience sells raw land, farm ground, and ranch.
3. **Never thank anyone for anything. Acknowledge instead.** "Glad you came back to me," not "thanks for getting back to me."
4. **Never suggest the agent explains, recommends, or drafts anything.** They are not licensed for it and the copy must never put them there.
5. **Say plainly that it does not fit everyone.** An agent who hears that first looks better later and keeps sending clients.
6. **All materials naming the structure require written approval before publishing**, submitted as an editable Word document.

---

## 4. Pre-send checklist

Run against every body and every subject line before it ships.

- [ ] No banned word from section 1 anywhere, including subject lines
- [ ] No structure named (rule 3.1)
- [ ] "Property," never "building" (rule 3.2)
- [ ] No thanking (rule 3.3)
- [ ] Nothing that puts the agent in an advisory role (rule 3.4)
- [ ] A "does not fit everyone" line is present (rule 3.5)
- [ ] No "pays you out," "payout," or "distribution." Repayments only
- [ ] No "invest," "reinvest," "stays invested," or "other investments"
- [ ] No dollar figure anywhere near a benefit
- [ ] Nothing implying a locked or future tax rate
- [ ] "Defer," never "avoid"

## 5. Carried over from earlier client calls (still in force)

Rob Lowe · audit statistics · indemnification · pricing · "secret method" · the unverified "70% of business owners fail" stat.

Compliance and deliverability pull the same direction here: rule 3.1 removes `deferred sales trust` and `opportunity zones` from cold copy, which were the only two things scoring Poor in the whole account.*
