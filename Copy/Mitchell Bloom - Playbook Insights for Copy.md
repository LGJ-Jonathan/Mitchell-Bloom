## ✅ Usable additions (all traceable to Mitch's own material)

### 1. "Timing and control, not avoidance" — the compliance-perfect trust beat
The playbook states it plainly: *"Deferring capital gains isn't about avoiding taxes — it's about timing and control,"* and *"you pay capital gains taxes only as you receive payments."* This is the single best line to add — it reinforces the non-promissory stance *and* builds trust with a financially literate reader.
**Proposed drop-in (new E2 or E3 variant):**
> To be clear, this is not about dodging the tax. You still pay it, just spread out as you receive payments instead of all at once at closing. That timing is the whole advantage.

### 2. Liquidity + diversification value lever (new angle — strongest for stockholders & commercial)
The playbook's headline DST benefit is liquidity and *diversifying proceeds across asset classes* — something we have not leaned on. Especially potent for the concentrated-stock ICP (get out of one position without the tax hit) and rental/commercial owners.
**Proposed drop-in (stockholders / commercial E1 or E3):**
> Selling a concentrated position usually means a big tax bill and starting over in the same asset. This defers the gain and lets the proceeds spread across other investments over time.

### 3. Sharper 1031 contrast (strengthens the existing E1-B "1031 reframe")
The playbook gives concrete 1031 limits: **45 days to identify, 180 days to close, real estate only, no diversification.** Our current E1-B just says a 1031 doesn't apply to a home. We can make the reframe bite harder.
**Proposed add to E1-B:**
> A 1031 also puts you on a 45 and 180 day clock and only rolls into more real estate. This does not.

### 4. Estate / legacy-to-heirs benefit (nurture / E3 angle)
Playbook: deferred assets "may pass more efficiently to heirs." This matches the intake avatar's stated desire for a **dynastic, multi-generational legacy** — a value lever for later-stage steps.
**Proposed drop-in (E3 nurture variant):**
> Structured right, the same trust can pass what is left to your heirs more efficiently than a taxed sale would.

### 5. Landlord-freedom emotional angle (rental / commercial vertical, not primary homes)
The Karen quote's usable *emotion* — "without the stress of being a landlord" — echoes the intake's "done with toilets, trash, tenants." Use the **feeling**, never the attributed testimonial. Best for the rental/commercial ICP, not trophy primary residences.
**Proposed drop-in (commercial/rental E1):**
> If you are ready to be done with the toilets, trash, and tenants but the tax bill at sale is what is stopping you, there is a way to defer it.
