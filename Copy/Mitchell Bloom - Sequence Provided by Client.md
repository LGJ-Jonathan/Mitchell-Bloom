*(Generic across all six strategies, adapted from the Deferred Sales Trust sequence)*

### Core Messaging Framework (used throughout)

**Opening hook** — lead with this in Email 1 and echo whenever reintroducing the topic:
> If you're the owner of a highly appreciated business, real estate, or a concentrated stock position, one of your biggest concerns is knowing that after your sale closes, you're going to owe capital gains tax.

**Old way vs. new way** — the core repositioning:
> **The old way:** you sell first. Afterward, your CPA runs the numbers and tells you how much you owe — capital gains tax, net investment income tax, and state income tax.
> **The new way:** you plan before you sell. You bring your intent to defer or mitigate capital gains tax to your CPA in advance, so the sale is structured correctly from the start, not reconstructed after the fact.

**Standard CTA line to your CPA** (educational emails):
> Bring this to your CPA before you sign anything. Let them know upfront that avoiding, deferring, or mitigating capital gains tax is part of the plan, not an afterthought.
