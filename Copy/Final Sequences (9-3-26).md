# Final Sequences (9/3/26)

Bloom Financial cold email sequences. Campaigns 1 and 2 for launch; Campaign 3 (business brokers) parked per Mitch. Prepared by Lead Gen Jay.

## How it works
- **Cadence:** Email 1 (Day 0) → Email 2 (Day 3, reply on same thread) → Email 3 (Day 7). Emails 2 and 3 send as replies on the Email 1 thread, so it reads as one conversation.
- **Rotation:** each contact gets one Email 1 version, not all. The system rotates them and keeps the ones earning the most interested replies. Small word-level variations (spintax) rotate inside each email for deliverability; versions shown are representative.
- **Auto-filled fields:** `[First Name]`, `[State]`, and `[Rate]` fill in per contact. `[Rate]` is the state-specific combined capital gains rate (Federal 20% + NIIT 3.8% + state), from the per-state tax doc. Signature is pulled from each sending mailbox.
- **CTA:** Emails 1 and 2 invite a quick 15-minute call. Email 3 is a soft close, offering to send an overview instead of asking for a call.

Signature + mailing address, an advertisement notice, a one-line opt-out, and the disclaimer. Shown once so the sequences read clean:
